Privacy and downloads.

The ArSonComp landing page is a static website. Its source does not embed advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, third-party fonts, or tracking cookies, and it does not ask for personal information.

Installer buttons open official GitHub Release downloads; GitHub may process those requests. We do not operate these services or control their logs.
