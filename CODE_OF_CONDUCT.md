# Community conduct

ArSonComp welcomes practical, technically grounded contributions from audio developers, DSP engineers, sound professionals and users.

- Be respectful. Do not harass, discriminate, intimidate or personally attack others.
- Critique ideas, code and evidence rather than people. Prefer reproducible examples and measurements over accusations.
- Respect contributor privacy and intellectual property. Do not publish private credentials, recordings or proprietary third-party code without permission.
- Keep pull requests and issues on topic, and disclose relevant conflicts or affiliations when appropriate.
- Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation to protect the community.

For interpersonal concerns, contact a project maintainer privately through an available platform channel; avoid posting private or sensitive complaints publicly. For exploitable software issues, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
